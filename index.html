About

I am a Lecturer in Cybersecurity at Southern Cross University. I received my PhD from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in 2021.

I build security tools that people can trust and actually use. My work spans security analytics, AI agent security, data privacy and vulnerability discovery. It appears at top security and software engineering conferences, including ACM CCS, NDSS, OOPSLA, FSE, ASE and ISSTA, and in top journals, including IEEE TIFS, IEEE TDSC, IEEE TSE, IEEE TMC and ACM TOSEM.

Research directions

Last updated: November 2026

1Post-detection security analytics automationDetection is only the start. I automate the analysis that follows an alert, so analysts can understand and respond to threats faster.

Work with me

Researchers

I welcome joint papers, grant applications and visits. Good fits: security analytics, agent security, data privacy, program analysis and fuzzing. Cross-disciplinary ideas are very welcome.

Industry

If your team runs a security operations workflow, deploys AI agents or shares sensitive data, I can help with security assessment, joint research and student projects. I am open to ARC Linkage style partnerships.
